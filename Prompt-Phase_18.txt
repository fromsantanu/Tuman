Phase 18 — Named Billing Rules and Attendance-Based Rule Selection

Allow a Teacher to maintain one fixed-monthly rule and multiple overlapping hourly rules for the same Student. Every rule has a clear name.

Invoice policy:
- Add an applicable fixed-monthly rule once for the billing month.
- An attendance entry with a selected hourly rule bills at that rule's historical rate.
- If a fixed-monthly rule exists, an attendance entry without an hourly selection adds no extra charge.
- If no fixed-monthly rule exists, an attendance entry without a selection automatically uses the highest active hourly rule for its date.
- Preserve invoice snapshots and validate Teacher ownership, assignment, effective dates, and currency consistency.
