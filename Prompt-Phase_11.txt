## Objective

Build the Teacher Payments module.

A Teacher must be able to record valid payments against only their own issued or partially-paid invoices, review payment history, and void an incorrect payment without deleting it. Payment writes and the resulting invoice balance/status must be one transaction.

Do not implement payment gateways, online checkout, receipts, email delivery, PDF generation, refunds, credits, overpayments, reports, Student self-service, or any Phase 12-or-later functionality.

## Payment and balance rules

Use these rules consistently in the service and user interface:

- A payment may be recorded only against a Teacher-owned invoice whose status is ISSUED or PARTIALLY_PAID.
- Never record a payment for DRAFT, CANCELLED, or PAID invoices.
- Payment date is required and must be a valid date.
- Amount is required, strictly greater than zero, has no more than two decimal places, and fits DECIMAL(12,2).
- Payment method must exactly match a supported method.
- Reference number is optional and limited to 100 characters.
- Remarks are optional and limited to 500 characters.
- New payments always begin as VALID. Do not accept payment status or voided_at from the browser.
- A new payment currency_code must be copied server-side from the owned invoice currency_code. Do not show a currency selector or accept a browser-provided payment currency.
- Payment amount, valid-payment sum, outstanding balance, and invoice total always use the same invoice currency. Reject any inconsistent stored payment currency safely; never convert currencies.
- The sum of VALID payments must never exceed the invoice total. Overpayments are not supported.
- Use integer minor units or validated decimal strings for all amount and balance calculations. Do not use PHP floats.
- A valid payment changes invoice status to PARTIALLY_PAID when the valid total is greater than zero but below the invoice total, or PAID when it equals the invoice total.
- If a valid payment is voided, set payment status to VOIDED and voided_at to UTC_TIMESTAMP(). Recalculate the invoice from remaining VALID payments: ISSUED when their total is zero, PARTIALLY_PAID when below invoice total, or PAID when equal.
- Never delete payments. A voided payment remains visible as history.
- Do not change invoice header totals or invoice-item snapshots in this phase.
- Display money with an unambiguous currency code, for example USD 300.00 or INR 2,000.00.

For every payment creation and void:

- Begin one transaction.
- Lock the owned invoice row using SELECT ... FOR UPDATE.
- Calculate the valid-payment total inside that transaction.
- Reject overpayment before inserting a payment.
- Insert/update the payment, update the invoice status, and write the audit entry in the same transaction.
- Roll back all writes if any validation, update, or activity-log step fails.

## Ownership and access rules

Every Teacher Payment page must enforce:

    tuman_require_role('TEACHER')

Every invoice/payment read or write must prove ownership through the linked assignment, conceptually:

    WHERE payment.id = :payment_id
      AND teacher_students.teacher_user_id = :current_teacher_id

and:

    WHERE invoice.id = :invoice_id
      AND teacher_students.teacher_user_id = :current_teacher_id

Do not trust invoice IDs, payment IDs, hidden fields, or URL parameters as authorization proof. A request for another Teacher’s invoice/payment must receive the same safe result as an invalid or missing record.

## Required Teacher functionality

Create a simple payments area, for example:

    teacher/payments.php
    teacher/payment-create.php
    teacher/payment-void.php

The invoice detail page may show its payments and link to these pages if that remains ownership-scoped.

### Payments list

Provide a page that lets the logged-in Teacher:

- Select one of their own invoices, with an option to see all their invoices/payments.
- See invoice number, student, payment date, amount, method, reference where available, payment status, voided date where applicable, and actions.
- Clearly distinguish VALID from VOIDED history.
- Record a payment for an eligible invoice.
- Void a valid payment.

Do not expose another Teacher’s invoices or payments.

### Record payment

Create a CSRF-protected POST form.

Required inputs:

    Invoice
    Payment date
    Amount
    Payment method

Optional inputs:

    Reference number
    Remarks

The invoice selector must include only Teacher-owned ISSUED/PARTIALLY_PAID invoices. Still recheck ownership, invoice state, currency, and balance server-side. Clearly show the invoice currency, total, amount paid, and outstanding balance before submission where practical, but never trust browser-calculated values.

### Void payment

Provide a CSRF-protected confirmation POST form.

Rules:

- Only the owning Teacher can void one of their VALID payments.
- Set status to VOIDED and voided_at in the service; do not delete the row.
- Recalculate and update the associated invoice status inside the same transaction.
- Clearly explain that the voided payment remains in history.
- A voided payment cannot be voided again.

Do not add payment editing. A correction is made by voiding the incorrect payment and recording a new one.

## Service design

Create a focused reusable service, for example:

    services/payments.php

It should centralize:

- Teacher-owned invoice and payment lookup.
- Payment/invoice listing and payment-history queries.
- Decimal-money validation, currency-aware display, and integer-safe amount conversion.
- Outstanding-balance and valid-payment-total calculation.
- Transactional payment creation, overpayment prevention, invoice-status updates, and activity logging.
- Transactional voiding and invoice-status recalculation.
- Ownership enforcement.

Reuse existing invoice money-display helpers only if they satisfy the no-float rules. Do not duplicate security-critical balance logic across pages. Use PDO prepared statements for all user-controlled values.

## Activity log

Use safe events:

    PAYMENT_RECORDED
    PAYMENT_VOIDED
    INVOICE_PAYMENT_STATUS_CHANGED

Log only minimal metadata, such as payment method, amount, currency_code, invoice number, and resulting invoice status. Do not log payment remarks, passwords, hashes, database credentials, or unnecessary personal information.

## Security and quality

- Every state-changing form uses POST and CSRF validation.
- Escape rendered values with htmlspecialchars().
- Use prepared statements and transactions.
- Never expose SQL errors, paths, credentials, or stack traces.
- Validate all identifiers, dates, payment state, and values server-side.
- Lock invoices before checking balances and writing payment changes.
- Retain all financial history; never delete a payment or mutate invoice snapshots.
- Do not add a framework or ORM.
- Do not implement Phase 12 or later functionality.

## Testing

Test at minimum:

    Unauthenticated user -> Payments page -> redirected
    Administrator -> Payments page -> denied
    Student -> Payments page -> denied
    Teacher -> Payments page -> allowed

Ownership:

    Teacher A -> own invoice/payment -> allowed
    Teacher A -> Teacher B invoice/payment -> denied
    Teacher A -> void Teacher B payment -> denied

Payment integrity:

    Valid payment below outstanding balance -> created, invoice becomes PARTIALLY_PAID
    Valid payment exactly matching outstanding balance -> created, invoice becomes PAID
    Payment exceeding outstanding balance -> rejected
    Payment on DRAFT/CANCELLED/PAID invoice -> rejected
    Invalid/zero/negative/over-precision amount -> rejected
    Invalid payment method/date/reference/remarks -> rejected
    Multiple valid payments -> accurate outstanding balance and status
    Valid payment is visible in payment history
    Payment currency_code is copied from the invoice and shown with the amount
    Payment against a USD/EUR invoice does not alter or convert its invoice currency
    Missing/invalid CSRF token -> rejected

Void and transaction history:

    Void a valid payment -> payment becomes VOIDED, voided_at is set
    Void partial payment -> invoice becomes ISSUED when no valid payments remain
    Void one of multiple payments -> invoice becomes PARTIALLY_PAID or PAID as appropriate
    Voided payment remains available in history
    Void already-voided payment -> rejected
    Failure rolls back payment/void, invoice status, and audit records together

Clearly distinguish PASS, BLOCKED, and NOT APPLICABLE.
